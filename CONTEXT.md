# Job Search

This context helps an individual discover and assess professional roles.

## Language

**Job Searcher**:
The individual whose LinkedIn account is used to find roles and whose professional profile may be promoted.
_Avoid_: User, candidate

**LinkedIn Account**:
The Job Searcher's authenticated account on LinkedIn.
_Avoid_: LinkedIn profile, session

**Job Searcher Profile**:
The structured professional information used to assess Positions for a Job Searcher. Its authoritative source is a CV supplied as a PDF or Markdown file.
_Avoid_: Profile, candidate profile

**Position**:
A role advertised by an employer on LinkedIn that may be relevant to the Job Searcher.
_Avoid_: Job, opening, listing

**Search Run**:
An on-demand request by the Job Searcher to discover and assess Positions.
_Avoid_: Crawl, scrape, refresh

**Match Criteria**:
The Job Searcher's explicit preferences and minimum AI-fit grade used to decide whether a Position is relevant enough to show.
_Avoid_: Filters, search settings

**Fit Grade**:
An AI assessment of a Position's relevance to the Job Searcher, expressed as `S`, `A`, `B`, `C`, or `D`; `S` is the strongest fit.
_Avoid_: Score, rating
